Haru Studio

The storytelling studio behind LOL Central

Haru Studio makes animated, manhwa-style stories told by a storyteller in everyday Urdu and Hindi, and publishes them to the LOL Central channels on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

The app is used only by the LOL Central team to upload our own videos to our own accounts. It does not collect data from the public.
