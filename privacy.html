Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

What we collect

Haru Studio runs on the LOL Central team's own computer. When our team connects our own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook account, the platform gives the app an access token and basic account information (such as the account name). That is all it receives.

How it is used

  • Only to upload videos our team has reviewed and approved to our own accounts.
  • Tokens are stored only on our own computer, never shared, sold or sent to anyone else.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect information from viewers or the public.
  • We do not read messages, followers' data or anything beyond what uploading needs.
  • We do not use data for advertising or sell it.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), and the stored token is deleted from our computer when an account is disconnected.

Children

Haru Studio is not used by children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Message us on the LOL Central YouTube channel or Facebook Page.
